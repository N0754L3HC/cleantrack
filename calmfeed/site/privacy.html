Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

What Calmfeed does

Calmfeed is a browser extension that hides selected posts from your LinkedIn feed. It reads the LinkedIn page in your browser only to decide which posts to hide.

Data we collect

None. Calmfeed does not collect, store, sell or send your LinkedIn data, browsing history, messages or profile to us or anyone else. Your settings (which filters are on, your muted keywords and a count of hidden posts) are stored only in your own browser using Chrome's local storage.

Payments

If you upgrade to Pro, payment is processed by ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) and Stripe. They receive your email address and payment details so they can take payment and manage your subscription. We never see your card details. See the ExtensionPay privacy policy and Stripe privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Access to linkedin.com: to hide posts on the page.
  • Storage: to save your settings in your browser.

Contact

Questions? Use the Support tab on Calmfeed's Chrome Web Store page.
