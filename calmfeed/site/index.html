Get Calmfeed

Calmfeed is in Chrome Web Store review. Want it today? Download it and install it in 30 seconds:

Download Calmfeed (.zip)
  1. Unzip the file.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Done. Open LinkedIn.

Questions

Is it safe? What do you collect?

Nothing. Calmfeed runs entirely in your browser and only reads the LinkedIn page to decide what to hide. It never sends your feed, messages or profile anywhere.

Will LinkedIn ban me?

No. Calmfeed only changes what your own browser displays. It doesn't automate anything, scrape anything or touch your account.

How do payments work?

Click "Go Pro" in the extension. Payment is handled securely by Stripe via ExtensionPay. Cancel any time from the same button.

Does it work on other browsers?

Any Chromium browser: Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.
