We'd rather show you than tell you.

Browser extensions can be scary, so here's exactly what Calmfeed can and can't do. Chrome enforces these limits, not us.

Calmfeed can

  • Read the LinkedIn page in your browser to decide which posts to hide
  • Save your filter settings on your own computer

Calmfeed can't

  • See any website other than linkedin.com
  • Post, like, message or do anything on your behalf
  • Send your feed, profile or messages to any server

Why we built this

We're a small independent team in the UK. Our LinkedIn feeds had turned into ads, engagement bait and posts from strangers, and finding updates from people we actually know took ages.

Calmfeed is the fix we wanted for ourselves: small, fast and private. The free version will stay free. Pro pays for us to keep up with LinkedIn's changes.

Get Calmfeed

Calmfeed is in Chrome Web Store review. Want it today? Download it and install it in 30 seconds:

Download Calmfeed (.zip)
  1. Unzip the file.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Done. Open LinkedIn.

Questions

Is it safe? What do you collect?

Nothing. Calmfeed runs entirely in your browser and only reads the LinkedIn page to decide what to hide. It never sends your feed, messages or profile anywhere. Read the privacy policy.

Will LinkedIn ban me?

No. Calmfeed only changes what your own browser displays. It doesn't automate anything, scrape anything or touch your account.

How do payments work?

Click "Go Pro" in the extension. Payment is handled securely by Stripe via ExtensionPay. You can cancel any time from the same place.

What if LinkedIn changes its layout?

LinkedIn updates its site often. We watch for changes and push fixes through Chrome automatically. You don't need to do anything.

Does it work on other browsers?

Any Chromium browser: Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc. Not yet on mobile.
